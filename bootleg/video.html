~ 1995


Milano Factory (VHS)
Japan
AIRS Rock Video (15170)

Earliest date possible: 07/28/1995
Latest date possible: 12/31/2000

Burnst's date estimate: August–December 1995

Details:

  • Contains video of 02/18/1995 concert
  • This bootleg appears on the Japanese fan site "Instant Weezer" as early as 02/05/2001
  • The Japanese bootleg store POP BEAT lists this bootleg in its "1994–2000" category; this suggests it is at least older than 2001
  • Earlier AIRS Video release SONIC YOUTH / RANDALLS ISLAND. N.Y. 7.29.95 (15108) was recorded 07/28/1995. Milano Factory - a later AIRS release by catalog number - must have released after this date
  • Per pattern of AIRS releases, the catalog number 15170 strongly suggests this bootleg was released during or before December 1995. See my research on AIRS Video for more information
