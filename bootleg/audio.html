~ 1995


Geezer (CD)
Australia
Kiwi Records (KTS-026-A)

Earliest date possible: 12/04/1994
Latest date possible: 04/14/1995

Burnst's date estimate: March/April 1995

Details:

  • Recognized by Karl Koch as the first commercially-released Weezer bootleg
  • Listed release date is 1995
  • Contains audio of 12/04/1994 concert
  • Listed for sale in Goldmine vol. 21 no. 8, published 04/14/1995

American Holiday (CD)
Italy (?)

[note]
No concrete evidence confirms that this bootleg was manufactured/released in Italy, although it is the most plausible origin. Disc was manufactured at a plant in Taiwan that was used globally for official and unofficial releases; including numerous European bootleg labels active in the mid-90s. Consensus online (as of my searching in 2026) is that Tornado operated out of Italy. Multiple Tornado releases ([1][2][3]) feature counterfit Italian SIAE stamps. The bootleg is known to have been sold in the United States as early as November 1995; I have not found a record of its sale in Italy (or Japan, or Taiwan), however this is moreso a result of the obscure and obfuscated nature of bootleg releases than evidence against Italian origin.


Tornado (TOR038 [artwork] / TOR-065 [disc])

Earliest date possible: 07/07/1995
Latest date possible: 11/09/1995

Burnst's date estimate: July 1995

Details:

  • Recognized by Karl Koch as the second commercially-released Weezer bootleg
  • Disc matrix includes string "950707", suggesting disc was manufactured on 07/07/1995
  • A fan posted that they had found the disc for sale at a PA record store in a Weezer-Rules mailing list message sent 11/09/1995


Rock Candy (CD) / Boys at Play (CD)

[note]
These two bootlegs are "sister" releases, in that they differ only in artwork and label. Both contain identical audio, were designed by the same group, and were produced under the parent label Sizzling Music. Both can be assumed to have identical or near-identical release dates.


Germany (?)

[note]
Artwork claims these were "Made in Germany" but Home Records, Troll Records, and the parent label Sizzling Music are all based in Italy (according to their entries on Discogs). It is possible they were made in Italy but claim to come from Germany to obfuscate their origin. It is also possible that the bootleg labels operated across borders in Europe.


Home Records (HR6037-1) / Troll Records (TR020) [Sizzling Music]

[note]
Sizzling Music is the Italian parent label of Home Records and Troll Records (among others). Both CDs had artwork designed by LAPIS. Rock Candy was distributed by Discolux Entertainment S.A.R.L..



Earliest date possible: 07/26/1995 (Rock Candy) • 09/09/1994 (Boys at Play)
Latest date possible: 02/24/1996 (Rock Candy) • 11/30/1998 (Boys at Play)

Burnst's date estimate: August–December 1995 (Rock Candy) • August–December 1995 (Boys at Play)

Details (Rock Candy):

  • Recognized by Karl Koch as the third commercially-released Weezer bootleg
  • Listed release date is 1995
  • Contains audio of 09/09/1994 concert
  • Bon Jovi - Christmas Benefit With Joe Satriani (HR6028-9) includes a track recorded July 26, 1995; consequently, Rock Candy (HR6037-1) must have been produced later than 07/26/1995
  • A fan posted that they owned the disc in a Weezer-Rules mailing list message sent 02/24/1996


Details (Boys at Play):

  • Likely shares a release date with "sister" release Boys at Play
  • Listed release date is 1995
  • Contains audio of 09/09/1994 concert
  • An alt.music.weezer user posted that they owned the bootleg on 11/30/1998

Japan (?)

[note]
Glass mastered at a Japanese plant used primarily by Japanese labels. Earliest evidence of its existence online is on a Japanese website.

[note]

Japan (?)

[note]

Loose Dog (LOOSE-019)

Earliest date possible: 06/08/1999
Latest date possible: 07/24/2000

Burnst's date estimate: July 2000

Details:

  • Includes Weezer's cover of "Velouria", which was first released 06/08/1999
  • The Japanese fan site "Instant Weezer" reported the release of the bootleg on 07/24/2000. Earlier, on 07/07/2000, it had announced that the release was upcoming and expected to be released by the end of the month
  • Shared online as a new release by the Japanese bootleg store POP BEAT in an August 2000 news webpage
  • A later POP BEAT webpage states that B-Sides and Rarities released "last summer just before their tour of Japan" [machine translated from Japanese]; Weezer's first 2000 show in Japan was 08/04/2000
  • This bootleg's entry on the dobroman5025 Weezer bootleg sale list includes the strings "EW000529" and "000529", suggesting it was released on or before 05/29/2000. I believe this is a mistake. The reports on "Instant Weezer" are quite specific about when the bootleg released. It also first arrived at POP BEAT in late July or early August. It may be the case that the author of the Instant Weezer blog recieved their information from POP BEAT themselves; as such, the July release date may refer to when the bootleg arrived at POP BEAT specifically, and not when it first entered circulation. If this is the case, it is possible other bootleg distributors (e.g., dobroman) may have had the bootleg as early as May. It is difficult to conclusively decide which source is more reliable. See my page on dobroman for additional thoughts
